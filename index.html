IBM Client Profile Agent

Team project with IBM interns · 2026

Consultants joining a new engagement often spend hours searching filings and news without knowing which details matter. Our team built an AI agent that produces a verified, one-page client profile from public sources, and I worked on the research behind it and how the agent thinks.

  • Took part in 19 interviews with IBM, Oracle, and SAP consultants and managers to learn which company, financial, leadership, and industry details matter most.
  • Wrote and refined the prompts and instructions that guide the agent's research and responses.
  • Defined tool requirements and expected behavior for retrieving public SEC filings, news, and company pages.
  • Helped design the research workflow that creates structured one-page profiles. In team testing it cut onboarding research from 2+ hours to under 2 minutes.
How it works

The agent runs on a local MCP server with 11 tools that pull public data and keep internal IBM systems out of reach. A PDF layout step enforces the one-page limit, and citations are only allowed from URLs the agent actually retrieved in that session. The team ran 24 automated test suites across 8 live organization profiles.
